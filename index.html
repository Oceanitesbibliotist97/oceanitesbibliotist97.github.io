Research

Research interests

I study how machines can understand 3D environments, generate feasible actions, and translate high-level design intent into executable manufacturing processes.

01

3D-Aware Robot Learning for Real-World Execution

Learning-based robotic assembly and manipulation with explicit 3D scene understanding and geometry-aware decision making.

  • 3D object understanding for robotic assembly
  • Constraint-aware trajectory generation
  • Online collision-aware motion planning
  • Real-time 3D scene representations
02

Generative Models for 3D Design & Manufacturing

Generative intelligence for automated conceptual design, spatial reasoning, part arrangement, and adaptive production.

  • Diffusion-guided 3D part assembly
  • Spatial reasoning and pose optimization
  • Design parameter optimization
  • Design-to-manufacturing automation
