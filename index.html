About

Engineering intelligence for the physical world.

I come from a mechanical engineering background, and that shapes how I approach AI research. I tend to think about learned systems not only in terms of prediction quality, but also in terms of geometry, constraints, feasibility, and execution.

My long-term interest is in adaptive robotic and manufacturing systems that can move beyond fixed scripts: systems that perceive a new scene or product, reason about what is possible, and generate an appropriate physical action.
