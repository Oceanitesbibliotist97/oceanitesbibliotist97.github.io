ABOUT

I care about intelligence that survives contact with the physical world.

A useful robot does more than predict a good next step. It needs to understand where things are, reason about what is physically possible, and produce motions that remain valid under geometric and kinematic constraints.

That is the thread connecting my research. I work on representations, generative models, and planning methods that bridge 3D perception → spatial reasoning → robot or manufacturing action.

I am especially interested in systems that can move beyond carefully scripted environments and adapt their decisions to the geometry of the scene, product, or task in front of them.
