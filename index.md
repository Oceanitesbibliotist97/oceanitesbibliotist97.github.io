## ✨ Key Features Everyone Will Appreciate

### 🚫 No Telemetry. Period.
Most software today quietly collects information about how you use it. Torrent doesn't. There is no tracking, no usage statistics, no "crash reports" sent to anyone. What you do on your computer stays on your computer.

### 🛡️ VPN Binding with Kill Switch
If you use a VPN (a service that hides your internet activity), Torrent can be set to only work when that VPN is active. If your VPN ever disconnects, Torrent **immediately stops all internet activity**. This prevents your real identity from ever being exposed, even for a split second.

SOCKS5 is a way to route your internet traffic through a proxy server. Some clients "leak" your real IP address even when using a proxy. Torrent's implementation is built to be fully leak-free, meaning your true location stays hidden when you enable this mode.

### 💼 Fully Portable
No installation needed. You can run Torrent from a USB stick, an external drive, or any folder. Your settings and downloads history stay with the program folder, not scattered across your system. Take your privacy with you anywhere.

## 🔐 Setting Up VPN Binding (Recommended for Privacy)

If you have a VPN:

1. Open **Settings** (the gear icon).
2. Go to the **"Connection"** or **"Network"** tab.
3. Find **"VPN Binding"** and select your VPN's network adapter from the drop-down list.
4. Check the box for **"Enable Kill Switch."**
5. Click **Save.**

Now Torrent will only communicate through your VPN. If the VPN drops, Torrent instantly stops all network activity until the VPN is back.

## 🧦 Using SOCKS5 Mode (Advanced Users)

For those using a SOCKS5 proxy:

1. Go to **Settings > Proxy.**
2. Select "SOCKS5" as the proxy type.
3. Enter your proxy address and port.
4. Check **"Leak Protection"** (it's on by default).
5. Click **Save.**

Your torrent traffic will now be routed through the proxy with no risk of your real IP leaking.
